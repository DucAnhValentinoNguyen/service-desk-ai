# Service Desk Escalation Policy

This synthetic safety policy is included for the service-desk demo.

Escalate requests when classification confidence is below 0.65, the request asks for an irreversible or protected change, identity is insufficient, the caller is abusive, or the knowledge base does not contain supporting evidence. Record the reason in the ticket timeline.
