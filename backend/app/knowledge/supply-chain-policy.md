# Supply Chain Reorder and Late PO Policy

This is a synthetic service-desk policy for the demo. It is informed by the building-automation context on the linked public source, not copied from that source.

For gateway batteries, the reorder point is 20 units. A purchase order more than 48 hours late is a supplier-risk exception. Service desk staff may draft an expedite request, but changing a purchase order requires operations manager approval.

## Inventory exceptions

Operators should check available stock, open purchase orders, and the promised delivery date before recommending an action.
